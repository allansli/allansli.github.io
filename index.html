Avanade · Lead Architect

Elo authorization core

Problem
A national card network needed its authorization core rewritten for cloud without giving up the latency the scheme requires.
Role
Lead Architect. Cloud-native Azure and AWS, ISO 8583, technical leadership of 20+ engineers.
Result
Authorization under 300ms and availability above 99.999%. TI Inside (opens in a new tab) reported, a year on, about 50% lower unit processing cost, 54% faster responses, and an 18× smaller financial impact from incidents.
  • ISO 8583
  • Authorization
  • High availability
  • Azure
  • AWS

CERC · Platform

Backstage and Apigee

Problem
Services and APIs were clear to the teams that owned them and opaque everywhere else.
Role
I own Backstage and Apigee as the reference for platformization: the developer portal and the API gateway.
Result
One catalog for engineering, and one governed edge for participants and internal systems, including calls into SHIFT from outside the cluster.

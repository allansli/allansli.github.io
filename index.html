Avanade · Payments

Elo core authorizer

Problem
A national card network needed its authorization core rewritten for cloud without giving up the latency the scheme requires.
Role
Lead Architect at Avanade. The architecture seat, separate from the Safrapay delivery-lead role.
Result
Authorization under 300ms and availability above 99.999% on a multicloud core. TI Inside (opens in a new tab) reported, a year on, about 50% lower unit cost per transaction, 54% faster responses, and an 18× smaller financial impact from incidents.
  • Multicloud
  • Authorization
  • High availability
  • Azure
  • AWS

CERC · Platform

Backstage and Apigee

Problem
Services and APIs at a regulated infrastructure were clear to the teams that owned them and opaque everywhere else.
Role
I set the platform architecture: Backstage as the developer portal and Apigee as the API gateway. Not the receivables ledger.
Result
One catalog for engineering, and one governed edge for participants and internal systems, including calls into SHIFT from outside the cluster.
  • Backstage
  • Apigee
  • Developer portal
  • API gateway
